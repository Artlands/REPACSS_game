Photon Dash

Every AI token starts as sunlight. Ride one photon all the way: off the Sun, through the West Texas sky, into the REPACSS solar array, down the power bus and into the cluster, until it comes out as a token.

  1. Leaving the Sun · dodge space dust
  2. West Texas sky · dodge clouds, dive into the 350 kW array
  3. Silicon to the power bus · now an electron: dodge hot resistors, fly through the rings
  4. The REPACSS cluster · weave past heat plumes, grab data packets
  5. Tensor cores · collect the tokens in order

Your energy drains as you fly. Collect glowing pickups to refill it, and fly through rings for bonuses.

Steer with the mouse, a finger, or arrow keys / WASD · P pauses
